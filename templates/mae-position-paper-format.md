# MAE Position Paper Standard Format

## Based on: Dr. Little's ENG 342 Position Paper Assignment

### Part 1: Position Paper (~1,200 words, APA)
Written in active collaboration with AI (MAE).

**Structure:**
1. **Introduction** — Rationale, overview, thesis statement
2. **Body** — Evidence supporting thesis + represent and refute at least one oppositional claim
3. **Conclusion** — Restate rationale, rearticulate thesis, call to action
4. **References** — APA, minimum 5 scholarly sources

### Part 2: Commentary (~800 words)
Written WITHOUT AI assistance.

**Covers:**
1. Distribution of labor between you and AI
2. Description of iterative prompt activity
3. Evaluation of the final draft
4. How the experience challenged/affirmed assumptions about writing and authorship

### Rules
- Both parts in one file
- APA citations throughout Part 1
- Commentary can reference course readings (cite in APA)
- This is the standard format for ALL MAE position papers going forward
