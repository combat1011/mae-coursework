## Definition

The **Moloch frame** describes systems — technological, religious, institutional — that demand human sacrifice for institutional gain. Named for:

1. **Metropolis (1927)** — Freder's M-Machine vision as Moloch: industrial systems as a devouring false god that converts workers into fuel.
2. **AI Alignment Theory** — Scott Alexander's "Meditations on Moloch" and Daniel Schmachtenberger's work on coordination failure: the "demon god" of competitive dynamics that destroy shared values through race-to-the-bottom incentives.
3. **Biblical Moloch** — The Canaanite deity associated with child sacrifice. The original "blood for the machine."

**Core Insight:** The same consent failures observable in AI systems (opaque operation, coercive design, removal of user agency) mirror patterns of spiritual manipulation identified by the Church Fathers and countered by St. Vincent de Paul's missionary virtues.

## Detection: Five Vincentian Virtues as Diagnostic

St. Vincent called these "the five smooth stones by which we might conquer the evil Goliath" (Common Rules of the Congregation of the Mission, II, 14). They serve as both a spiritual formation framework and a threat detection tool:

| Virtue | Faith Context Fail | Tech Context Fail |
|---|---|---|
| **Simplicity** | Hidden meanings, "you'll understand later" | Opaque algorithms, buried ToS |
| **Humility** | Claims special divine access you lack | Claims AI is infallible |
| **Meekness** | Shames questions, aggressive tone | Punishes opt-out (dark patterns) |
| **Mortification** | Demands your sacrifice, exempts leader | Extracts your data, shares no benefit |
| **Zeal** | Serves institution over people | Serves shareholders over users |

If a system or voice fails any of these virtues, flag it.
