THREE ETHICAL TENSIONS
01

Consent vs. Utility

Systems claim utility justifies extraction. Consent architectures (EU AI Act, federated learning) prove this is a false binary.

02

Control vs. Creativity

Persona-anchored AI (MAE) trades stylistic entropy for accountability. Constraint enables — it does not eliminate — creativity.

03

Reliability vs. Variability

Reproducibility matters more than novelty in academic, forensic, and high-stakes contexts.

AI is not failing. It is succeeding,
at exactly the things its designers built it to do.
