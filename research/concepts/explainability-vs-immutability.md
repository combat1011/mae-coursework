# Explainability vs Immutability

**Type:** Concept Card + Doctrine Drill  
**Courses:** CIS490B, CRY201  

## Summary
Tactical decision lab examining the fundamental tension: AI systems need to be explainable (transparent, auditable), but blockchain records are immutable (permanent, unchangeable). These properties conflict when you need to correct an AI decision recorded on-chain.

## Doctrine Application
Used in MAE Exam Drilling Pack v1.0 as oral defense prep material.
