# Trustless Systems + Human Failsafes

**Type:** Concept Card  
**Courses:** CRY201, CIS490B  
**Cross-Links:** AI Ethics, Crypto doctrine

## Summary
Explores the tension between trustless computational systems (blockchain, smart contracts) and the human failsafes needed when autonomous systems make harmful decisions. When an AI operating on a DAO makes a choice that harms users, who is responsible?

## Key Questions
- How do trustless architectures handle accountability?
- What human override mechanisms are needed?
- How does MAE's single-commander model contrast with decentralized governance?
